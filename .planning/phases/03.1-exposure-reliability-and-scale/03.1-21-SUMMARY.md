## Decisions Made

- The permanent typed-image ontology contract is approved as version 2. Its stable identity scheme is scoped and content-addressed. Any future reinterpretation requires a new additive vocabulary version, migration/backfill of retained generations, dual-reader compatibility during rollout, relationship regeneration, and coordinated rollback of the active generation pointer. Reverting code alone is not a valid data rollback.
- `ExposureReadPort` is the authority boundary for every newly wired consumer. Scope filtering therefore occurs before ontology construction, count/source aggregation, remediation handoff generation, or Copilot output.
- The new remediation path is intentionally read-only. A container finding can expose exact-digest review context but cannot create or invoke an executable action.
