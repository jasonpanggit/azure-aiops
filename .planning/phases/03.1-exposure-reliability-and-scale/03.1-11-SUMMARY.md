## Decisions Made

- The accepted ontology v2 vocabulary remains unchanged. Stable ontology IDs are scoped by tenant and subscription and use the already-durable Fabric key as the identity input.
- The reverse adapter reads only active typed entities and rejects conflicting duplicate Fabric keys, preventing retired generations or divergent payloads from silently replacing current evidence.
- Workload traversal requires typed edge triples plus exact Fabric keys, digest equality, event interval, provenance, effective generation, checkpoint identity, and complete non-carried-forward evidence.
- Unknown coverage contributes to `total_count`, `excluded_count`, and `excluded_reasons`; it never creates a deployment edge or inferred workload identity.
- A Fabric update exception is treated as reconciled only when the requested definition is observably active. Otherwise the prior definition is restored and verified before the error is re-raised.
