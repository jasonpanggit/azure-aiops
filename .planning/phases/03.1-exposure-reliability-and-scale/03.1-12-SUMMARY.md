## Decisions Made

- Gold `fact_id` includes connection/scope, target kind and identity, weakness, source family, source state, and generation; `scope_key` deliberately excludes generation so authorized scope identity remains stable across refreshes.
- The granular Plan 11 container product is authoritative for image facts. Legacy temporal image rows are not duplicated into the Gold server projection.
- Only Plan 23 durable records are eligible for enrichment joins. Gold does not accept a resolver or provider client and drops malformed/unsafe records while counting the rejection in reconciliation metadata.
- Healthy-empty authorized scopes retain zero-count summaries instead of disappearing, and authorized subscriptions can contribute secure score even when they currently have no exposure fact.
- Gold Delta rows remain generation-qualified and retained. A small CAS-protected pointer selects the readable generation, so rollback changes only the pointer.
