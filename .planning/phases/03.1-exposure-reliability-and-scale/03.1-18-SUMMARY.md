## Decisions Made

- The metric schema is closed at both dimension-name and dimension-value levels. Unknown source or operation values cannot silently create new time series.
- Ingestion drop is measured against effective LKG rows, so a failed source carrying a valid LKG does not look like a false clear; attempted and effective rows remain separately visible.
- Workload checkpoint telemetry is aggregated across workspaces and connections into the bounded `workloads` source family. No workspace, connection, target, or subscription identity reaches metrics.
- Shadow comparison is symmetric: old-only and new-only rows are both reported. Neither reader is designated as truth.
- Gold shadow failure writes a `shadow_blocked` generation manifest with redacted sample tokens, then raises before the readable pointer changes.
- The default promotion policy is zero unexpected mismatches and zero unexpected ratio. Nonzero tolerance is explicit and still cannot override hard-failure classes.
- Alert windows equal the configured ingestion cadence (30 minutes by default); evaluation runs every five minutes by default and cannot be configured slower than ingestion.
