## Decisions Made

- Portal links are never assembled from tenant, resource, provider, or route fragments in the client. The server must provide the complete URL, and the client independently validates it against the closed action registry and authorized row identity.
- Defender-specific rows continue to come from their existing typed endpoints, but each independent request is paired with `exposureOverview` using the canonical scoped query. This adds the unified contract without changing the backend or Phase 11-owned orchestration surfaces.
- Resource actions require both exact ARM equality and membership in the row-derived authorized resource set. Registry and cluster actions additionally require their exact resource provider/type.
- A container `eligible` outcome is treated as unsafe rather than relabeled. Only an explicit `review_handoff` opens governed remediation; absent, malformed, or executable outcomes produce `no_safe_action`.
- Source-state copy uses represented/expected connection counts, while common-contract finding counts are separately rendered with explicit units to avoid mixing the two denominators.
