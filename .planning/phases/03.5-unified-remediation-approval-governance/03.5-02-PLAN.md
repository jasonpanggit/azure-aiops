<objective>
Split "record an approval decision" from "execute/retry/cancel approved
work" into two distinct RBAC actions (D-03), so a principal can hold one
without the other. Today both are gated on the single `operate` action
(`src/api/routers/operations.py:361`, `src/api/routers/remediation.py`
execute/retry/cancel/supersede routes all use `OperatePrincipal`) — this
plan adds the machinery (`approve` action, `ROLE_APPROVER`,
`ApprovePrincipal`) without yet rewiring any route (that is Plan 03.5-06,
after the shared gate and requirement-satisfaction logic exist to give the
new route a reason to change together).

Purpose: makes "approve-only" and "operate-only" principals expressible
and enforceable at the RBAC primitive level — the foundation Plan 03.5-06
switches `record_remediation_decision` onto.
Output: `ACTION_APPROVE`, `ROLE_APPROVER`, `require_approve`/`ApprovePrincipal`,
and tests proving the four-way separation (approve-only, operate-only,
admin-both, and the unchanged reader/write behavior).
</objective>
