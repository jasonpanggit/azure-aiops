<objective>
Bring the live patch and live Defender-configuration planners onto the same
evidence-sensitive `action_version_for()` helper (D-04) and the same
`ApprovalEngine`-shaped `approval_requirements` rows (D-01) that the
Fabric/catalog planner already uses via `RemediationGenerator`. Today
`live_operations.py`'s `_plan()` computes `action_version` from
`{action: recommended_action, rollback: [...]}` alone (no target id, no
evidence digest) and `_configuration_plan()` computes it from
`{action_id, target, recommendation, assessment_id}` — two different
formulas, neither matching the Fabric planner's, and neither running
through the shared helper this phase requires (REVIEW recommendation 4:
"live_operations.py:607-611 vs remediation_generator.py:96-101 (divergent
action_version)"). Both methods also hand-roll a single-row
`{"role": ..., "reason": ...}` `approval_requirements` list instead of the
machine-checkable `ApprovalEngine.requirements()` shape Plan 03.5-03's
evaluation engine expects to consume.

Purpose: "one evidence-sensitive approval policy across patch and Defender
action families" (phase goal) is not achievable while the live path's two
action families compute action_version and approval_requirements two
different, ad hoc ways.
Output: `_plan()` and `_configuration_plan()` both call
`action_version_for()` and both emit `ApprovalEngine.requirements()`-shaped
rows.
</objective>
