| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-036-08 | Information Disclosure | Verify-step failure_details rendering | low | accept | `failure_details` already flows through the existing, already-shipped execution record contract (Phase 03.2-03.4); this plan only changes which of two locked sentences wraps it, and never renders a raw stack trace -- unchanged trust posture from prior phases. |
| T-036-09 | Spoofing | Unrecognized blocker `code` values | low | mitigate | The client never trusts an unrecognized code to mean anything beyond "an unrecognized workflow condition is blocking this step" -- it never infers severity/next-action beyond the safe fallback, and always tells the operator to contact the platform team rather than guessing at meaning. |
