<objective>
Make the topology and analysis contracts bounded and honest at enterprise scale, without changing what today's
callers receive.

Purpose: RESEARCH measured the real cost. The topology endpoint materialises every NSG rule, every route, and
every firewall rule as its own node with no cap, no pagination, and no detail parameter: 34,600 nodes and 78.70 MB
at enterprise size. Gaps and rule findings are sliced at the server default with no truncation signal, so at
enterprise scale the UI shows 0.14 percent of findings and agents show 0.03 percent, both presented as complete.

RESEARCH also corrected the review's target and this plan follows the correction, not the original: dropping
rule-level nodes alone yields 130 nodes but 729.4 KB, because surviving NSG and route-table nodes embed their
entire rule and route collections. The 250 KB gate is reached at 151.3 KB only when the detail contract also
projects a lean node payload. Pitfall P-1 is explicit that node count is the visible metric and embedded detail is
invisible until serialisation, so lean payload is a first-class part of this task, not a follow-up.

Locked decision 6 and pitfall P-8 govern compatibility: the detail parameter is additive and defaults to today's
behaviour, and the default is not flipped within this phase.

Output: a bounded, additive, drill-down-backed contract with measured budgets.
</objective>

    - At summary detail the payload contains no NSG-rule, route, or firewall-rule nodes (REC-3 AC-3, A7).
    - At summary detail, surviving NSG and route-table nodes carry no embedded rule, route, or rule-collection collections.
    - Gate G-2: on the enterprise fixture, summary detail reports a truncated flag and applies a node cap; no response exceeds an explicit byte ceiling.

    resolve against the scoped service so it cannot be used to read a resource the caller could not otherwise see.

    While in the projection, fix the double computation RESEARCH verified: the projection runs twice per response
    because the relationship builder recomputes it to derive its resource id set. Compute once and pass it
    through. Beyond the wasted work at these payload sizes, two independent projections could diverge (IN-01).
