## The Priority Weight Table

Research Open Question Q2 asked whether to rebalance or to bolt a Defender term onto a budget with no headroom. The planner decision is a full rebalance to a clean 100-point budget with business impact as the largest term. This replaces the current buckets, which summed to 110 before an outer clamp.

Bucket | Points | Source
--- | --- | ---
`business_impact` | 35 | The business impact score produced by `ImpactEstimator`, scaled. Zero when the business mapping is unknown.
`defender_exposure` | 18 | Active critical and high alerts up to 8, attack-path membership and target position up to 6, unhealthy controls and secure-score gap up to 4.
`expected_risk_reduction` | 15 | Simulated risk reduction percent, scaled.
`service_criticality` | 12 | Business criticality band.
`dependency_count` | 8 | Bounded dependency count.
`lifecycle_risk` | 5 | Lifecycle risk score, scaled.
`patch_risk` | 5 | Patch risk score, scaled.
`incident_history` | 2 | Incidents in the trailing 90 days, bounded.
**Total** | **100** | Priority bands stay 75, 50, and 25.

**The structural guarantee.** When the business mapping is unknown, `business_impact` contributes zero. Every remaining bucket sums to 65, which is below the 75 threshold for the top band. A finding therefore cannot reach Priority 1 on technical evidence alone; it can only get there by way of evidenced business impact. This is D-04 and D-09 enforced by arithmetic rather than by a convention someone can later forget.

## Trust Boundaries
