<objective>
Turn the remediation surface from a list of things to fix into a decision workspace that explains why, why not, what it costs either way, and what else could be done, and make approvals honest about evidence that has moved underneath them.

Purpose: This is where the locked correction becomes visible to a human. If the platform cannot explain a recommendation in business terms on screen, it has not delivered the objective regardless of what the backend computed.
Output: A decision-first remediation surface, an audit-readable decision route, evidence-aware approval freshness, and coverage proving approvals still work unchanged.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| API response -> browser rendering | Decision narrative and evidence citations derived from vendor data are rendered to operators. |
| Operator input -> approval persistence | A human decision crosses into a durable audit record. |
