<objective>
Make business consequence, not finding closure, the thing the platform reasons about. Turn a correlated Defender case into a decision an operator can defend: what happens if we act, what happens if we do not, why now or why later or why never, and what the alternatives cost.

Purpose: The user's locked correction is that remediation is not finding closure and technical severity alone must never decide priority. This plan is where that correction becomes executable logic rather than intent.
Output: A business decision engine, an alternatives comparison, and generated plans that carry urgency rationale, secure-score improvement, and explicit uncertainty.
</objective>
