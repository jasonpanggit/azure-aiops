<objective>
Generalize the Defender-family-only freshness/status/quality pattern already proven in `VulnerabilityIntelligenceService._coverage_for_family()` and `src/api/runtime.py::_snapshot_failure_table()` into one dataset-agnostic Fabric medallion health contract, implementing MED-03 in full.

Purpose: Every later Phase 10 surface (the dedicated health API, the web tab, Data Agent grounding's freshness-SLO guardrail) needs one canonical answer to "what is this dataset's status" — reusing the Defender-only implementation everywhere else would recreate exactly the copy-paste risk RESEARCH.md's "Don't Hand-Roll" section warns against.
Output: `src/fabric/medallion_health.py::dataset_status(table_name)` returning `{availability, freshness, quality, row_count, last_success_at, lineage_ref}`; a new per-dataset freshness-SLO config section.
</objective>
