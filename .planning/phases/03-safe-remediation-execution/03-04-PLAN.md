<objective>
Move the execution safety boundary to approval time. Persist the exact server-derived patch action that the approver reviewed, define deterministic business-impact eligibility, and remove the parallel direct patch mutation API.

Purpose: Selecting patches only after approval lets newly discovered patches enter an approved action, while caller-parameterized patch runs bypass Phase 2's evidence and business decision entirely.
Output: An immutable approval-bound action snapshot that is the sole source of execution parameters.
</objective>

Preview returns the full candidate snapshot and hash. During approved controlled-patch-recovery decisions, recompute the candidate under operate scope immediately before append and require an exact constant-time hash match; drift returns typed `stale_execution_preview` and persists nothing.
